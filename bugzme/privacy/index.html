BugzMe

Privacy Policy
Developer: LPZ Appz
Last updated: September 17, 2026

Privacy

BugzMe is designed to work locally on your Android device. BugzMe does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

Installed apps

When you open Important Apps, BugzMe reads the list of launchable apps installed on your device so it can show choices for you to select. The installed-app list is processed locally and is not sent to LPZ Appz or any third party.

Notification access

BugzMe asks for Android Notification Access so it can detect notifications from apps you choose, determine whether an original watched notification is still active, and remind you again until that original notification is cleared. Notification information is processed on your device and is not sent to LPZ Appz or any third party.

Local settings

Your selected-app list and BugzMe settings are stored locally in the app's private storage. The Play release disables Android app backup for BugzMe, so these settings are not intentionally copied to cloud backup by the app.

Network, ads, analytics, and accounts

BugzMe has no Internet permission and uses no account system, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, tracking SDK, or subscription service.

Permissions and special access

BugzMe may request notification permission, Notification Access, vibration, audio-setting control for the optional Normal/Loud reminder modes, and Alarms & reminders access for precise screen-off reminder timing. These are used only for BugzMe's reminder functions.

Data sharing and deletion

BugzMe does not share personal data because it does not transmit personal data off the device. You can remove locally stored BugzMe settings by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling BugzMe.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact LPZ Appz through the public GitHub profile at github.com/LPZ-Appz.
