What BugzMe does

BugzMe lets you choose important apps and repeatedly reminds you while an original watched notification is still active. The reminder cycle ends when the original watched notification is cleared or removed.

Permissions

BugzMe needs Android Notification Access to watch selected notifications. Precise screen-off reminder timing may also use Android's Alarms & reminders access. The app may request notification and vibration-related permissions for reminder delivery.
