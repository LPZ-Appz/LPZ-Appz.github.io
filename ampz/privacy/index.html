Ampz Live Charge Widget

Privacy Policy
Developer: LPZ Appz
Last updated: September 15, 2026

Privacy

Ampz Live Charge Widget does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

Device information used by the app

The app reads charging current and charging voltage locally on your device only to calculate and display live charging amps, volts, and watts in the home-screen widget. This information is not sent off your device.

No accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking

Ampz does not use user accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking technologies, location access, or internet access.

Charging service

When an Ampz widget exists, a minimal foreground service remains available to respond to charger connect and disconnect events. While the device is unplugged, Ampz does not continuously poll charging telemetry. While charging, the widget refreshes approximately once per second.

Children's privacy

Ampz is a general-purpose utility and is not designed specifically for children. The app does not collect personal information from users of any age.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact LPZ Appz through the public GitHub profile at github.com/LPZ-Appz.
